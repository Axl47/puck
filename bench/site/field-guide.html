Field Guide

Before You Start

Lay out every part on a clean cloth before you open the housing. Count the screws twice; the small ones roll.

The Lantern Rule

Never trust a flame you can’t see the base of — if the wick sits below the collar, trim it until the glow reaches the glass evenly, then wait ninety seconds before closing the hood. A lantern that smokes in the first minute will smoke all night.

Storage

Drain the reservoir before storing a lantern for more than a month, and keep the glass wrapped.
